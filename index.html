Fight the way they fought

Regiments, not units

Men march and fight as formed bodies. Get the drill right and the line holds. Get it wrong and it runs.

  • Lines, columns and squares, with officers, drummers and colours.
  • Musketeers fire by ranks and reload. Pikes stand against horse.
  • Morale matters. Men waver, run and rally. When the flag goes down, the regiment is broken.
  • The charge. Hit a line in the flank with hussars and watch it fold.

Play a friend

Two phones on the same Wi-Fi. One hosts, the other joins, in Skirmish or Battle.

  • Fair: card levels and saved generals don't count. Same rules for both.
  • No account, no chat. Just the two of you and the field.

Made for touch

One finger pans. A tap gives an order. Press and drag to draw a battle line.

  • Army bar: every regiment one tap away.
  • A five-minute lesson teaches a skirmish step by step.
  • Works offline. Three graphics levels for older phones and big tablets.
