01 / Build your force

Your army.
Your line.

Arrange up to eight regiments across the front, wings and second line. Keep the veterans you trust, call up owned reserves, and see the cost of every loss.

  • Collect regiments from every nation and mix them in one army.
  • Fill empty places or build your strongest line with one tap.
  • Keep your survivors. Refill casualties and earn veterancy across battles.
