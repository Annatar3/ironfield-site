IRONFIELD

Pike, shot and winged hussars. Real-time strategy of the 1600s, built for your phone.

Coming soon to Google Play

The game

  • Skirmish: build a town, feed it, mine iron and gold, raise regiments and break the enemy.
  • Battles of pike and musket, cannon and cavalry: volleys by rank, charges that strike home, morale that breaks.
  • A campaign across Poland-Lithuania, Sweden, Muscovy and the Ottoman Empire.
  • Build your army from every nation, hire black-clad mercenaries, raise your own general.
  • Play a friend on the same Wi-Fi.
