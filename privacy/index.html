Ironfield — Privacy policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026. This policy covers the Android game Ironfield (com.ironfield.skirmish), published by Demch (contact: nikayroo@gmail.com).

What the game stores

Your progress (profile, cards, generals, campaign, settings) is saved only on your device. Android may include these files in your own Google account backup so they return when you reinstall the game. We do not run servers and do not receive your saves. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases.

Playing with a friend

Local multiplayer connects directly to another device on the same Wi-Fi network. Only game orders are exchanged between the two devices; nothing is sent to us.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from Profile → Privacy & ads in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect your name, email, contacts or precise location.

Children

Ironfield is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes its saves from the device. For data held by Google for advertising, use your Android ad settings or Google's tools.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. Questions: nikayroo@gmail.com.
