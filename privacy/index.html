Ironfield — Privacy policy

Effective date: 7 October 2026. This policy covers the Android game Ironfield (com.ironfield.skirmish), published by Demch (contact: nikayroo@gmail.com).

What the game stores

Your progress (profile, cards, generals, campaign, settings) is saved on your device. Android may include these files in your own Google account backup so they return when you reinstall the game. We do not receive your saves. The game has no user accounts, no chat and no in-app purchases.

Cloud save (Google Play Games)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game keeps one saved game in your Google account (your profile, campaign and stars) so your progress survives a new phone. Google stores it under Google's privacy policy; we cannot read it. You can delete it from the Play Games app or your Google account.

Playing online

Online matches (Quick match and room codes) go through our match server at play.ironfield.site, which runs on Cloudflare. The server pairs two players and passes their match messages between them. It handles your commander name, the game's version, the mode, a room code if you use one, and the match itself (your nation, army and orders). Like any internet connection, it also sees your IP address.

The server keeps a match only in memory while it is played (a dropped connection can come back within 60 seconds; a match ends after 3 hours at most). Nothing about the match is written to a database, we keep no logs of players or matches, and there are no accounts. The other player sees your commander name, nation, army and orders. Cloudflare processes the connections to deliver the service and protect it from abuse, under Cloudflare's privacy policy.

Friends

When the game is open on its menus it connects to the same server (play.ironfield.site) so you can have friends. The first time, the server makes you a player record: a random player id, a secret key (the server keeps only a scrambled copy of it), a six-letter friend code, your commander name and nation, when you were last online, and the friends you added. Your friends see your commander name, nation, friend code, whether you are online or in a battle, and when you were last online. A friend can challenge you to a match; nothing else passes between you, and there is no chat.

You can delete your player record at any time: Friends → Delete my friend code in the game removes it, your code and your friendships from the server at once. A player record not used for 400 days is deleted automatically.

Playing nearby

Nearby (local Wi-Fi) play connects directly to another device on the same network. Only game orders, your commander name, nation and army are exchanged between the two devices; nothing is sent to us. While you host, other devices on the network can see that a game is open and its commander name.

Advertising

The game shows ads through Google AdMob, including optional rewarded videos. AdMob may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, IP address and approximate location, device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from Profile → Privacy & ads in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's privacy policy.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not collect your real name, email, contacts or precise location; the only thing we keep about you is the player record described under Friends. We do not use analytics or tracking of our own.

Children

Ironfield is intended for players aged 16 and over and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game deletes its saves from the device. Our match server keeps nothing after a match ends. Your player record (friends) is deleted with Friends → Delete my friend code, or write to us. For data held by Google for advertising, use your Android ad settings or Google's tools.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the game's data practices change. Questions: nikayroo@gmail.com.
